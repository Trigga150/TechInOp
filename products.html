DAI, HAI & RAI

Three pillars of operational AI—designed for homes, vehicles, and robots, and connected through a secure intelligence fabric.

DAI – Domestic AI

Intelligence at Home, In Your Hands

DAI brings adaptive intelligence into the spaces where you live and work. From smart assistants to wearables and domestic robots, DAI learns your routines and orchestrates your environment around comfort, safety, and efficiency.

  • Smart home assistants that coordinate lighting, security, climate, and entertainment.
  • Context‑aware wearables that understand activity, environment, and wellbeing signals.
  • Domestic robots that support cleaning, caregiving, and daily logistics.
  • Energy optimization to reduce waste while maintaining comfort and safety.
DAI Home Demo

Use Cases

Energy‑Aware Homes

DAI predicts usage patterns and dynamically tunes devices to save power without compromising comfort.

Assistive Caregiving

Domestic robots and sensors support caregivers with reminders, monitoring, and routine tasks.

Lifestyle Automation

From morning routines to focus modes, DAI synchronizes your environment to your day.

HAI – Hybrid Automotive AI

Smarter Journeys, Safer Roads

HAI powers the next generation of mobility with autonomous capabilities, predictive maintenance, and connected systems that learn from real‑world conditions.

  • Autonomous driving tuned for dense, unpredictable environments.
  • Predictive maintenance that monitors components and flags issues before they fail.
  • Fleet intelligence for logistics, ride‑share, and commercial operators.
  • Connected mobility linking vehicles, infrastructure, and cloud analytics.

Use Cases

Urban Autonomy

Advanced perception and planning for city driving, intersections, and mixed traffic.

Smart Fleets

Optimization across routes, charging, maintenance, and driver scheduling.

Connected Corridors

Vehicles that cooperate with infrastructure for smoother, safer travel.

RAI – Robotic AI

Robots That Work, Explore & Care

RAI enables robots to operate in demanding environments—from factory floors to disaster zones and hospitals. It focuses on robust perception, control, and coordination across fleets of heterogeneous robots.

  • Industrial automation with high‑precision control and safety‑first collaboration.
  • Exploration robotics for hazardous, remote, or unstructured environments.
  • Service robots that assist in logistics, healthcare, and public spaces.
  • Swarm intelligence for coordinated multi‑robot missions.

Use Cases

Next‑Gen Manufacturing

High‑throughput lines with human‑safe cobots and adaptive quality control.

Disaster Response

Robots navigating unstable structures and hazardous zones to support first responders.

Care & Service

Robots that deliver supplies, assist staff, and support patients and guests.
